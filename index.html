🪙 Oregon Coast Gold Leads

Legal disclaimer — read before going

  • ARPA / archaeology: The Archaeological Resources Protection Act (16 USC 470ee) and ORS 358.905–.961 make it illegal to dig or remove artifacts or disturb historic/prehistoric sites (incl. old mine workings, cabins, homesteads >50–100 yrs) on federal, state or tribal land. Felony penalties.
  • Private land: Most upland sites are private timber or residential land. Written landowner permission is required; trespass is a crime. Check active mining claims (BLM MLRS) — claim jumping is illegal.
  • State parks / ocean shore: Oregon Parks (OPRD) rules (OAR 736-010/021) prohibit digging, metal-detector excavation and mining in parks without permit; ocean-shore gold panning only with hand tools in limited areas. Cape Perpetua Scenic Area (USFS) & Heceta Head are protected.
  • Salmon streams: Motorized mining is banned in ESH salmon habitat (SB 3, ORS 517.123). Hand panning only, within the ODFW in-water work window; DSL removal-fill rules apply. No digging in banks.
  • Locations: Lidar candidates are unverified terrain features; newspaper leads are APPROXIMATE. Ownership labels are rough—verify with county GIS. Coastal cliffs are dangerous.
