🪙 Oregon Gold Leads PUBLIC BETA

A field map of Oregon gold prospecting leads. It covers the central coast (Yachats, Cape Perpetua, Heceta Head, Whiskey Run) and SW Oregon gold country (Josephine, Jackson, Curry, Douglas and Coos counties). It brings together:

  • Lidar candidates: pads, pits and ledges found in USGS 3DEP elevation data
  • Recorded mines from USGS MRDS and DOGAMI MILO-4, clustered, with land status from BLM Surface Management Agency data
  • Historic mining districts: approximate centers with short sourced history
  • Newspaper leads from 1853–1931 news reports (locations are approximate)
  • Historic homesteads

Filter by confidence and land status, tap a marker for photos, sources and directions, and use 📍 to find yourself in the field.

Legal disclaimer & terms of use

Informational and entertainment use only. This is not legal advice. Oregon Gold Leads shows historical research and computer-detected terrain features. A marker does not mean there is gold, that you may access the site, or that prospecting there is legal.

No trespassing implied. Many sites are on private land. Showing a site on this map gives no permission to enter it. Get written permission from the landowner first.

  • ARPA / archaeology: The Archaeological Resources Protection Act (16 USC 470ee) and ORS 358.905–.961 make it illegal to dig or remove artifacts or disturb historic/prehistoric sites (incl. old mine workings, cabins, homesteads >50–100 yrs) on federal, state or tribal land. Felony penalties.
  • Private land: Most upland sites are private timber or residential land. Written landowner permission is required; trespass is a crime. Check active mining claims (BLM MLRS) — claim jumping is illegal.
  • State parks / ocean shore: Oregon Parks (OPRD) rules (OAR 736-010/021) prohibit digging, metal-detector excavation and mining in parks without permit; ocean-shore gold panning only with hand tools in limited areas. Cape Perpetua Scenic Area (USFS) & Heceta Head are protected.
  • Salmon streams: Motorized mining is banned in ESH salmon habitat (SB 3, ORS 517.123). Hand panning only, within the ODFW in-water work window; DSL removal-fill rules apply. No digging in banks.
  • Land status comes from BLM Surface Management Agency data (lookup Oct 2026). It is generalized and does not show private inholdings, patented claims or active mining claims. Before you dig, check active claims in BLM MLRS.
  • Locations: Lidar candidates are unverified terrain features; newspaper leads are APPROXIMATE. Ownership labels are rough—verify with county GIS. Coastal cliffs, sneaker waves and tides are dangerous.
